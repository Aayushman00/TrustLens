# Round 2 of the evidence review: independent reviewers (checklist)

Why: round 1 had seven submissions, but six used the same code (`R0`), so they cannot be treated as six
independent people. Round 2 fixes that. Target: 3 to 5 reviewers, none of whom built TrustLens.

1. **Pick reviewers** who did not work on the probes, thresholds or O/S/D mapping (classmates from other
   projects, labmates). Do not coach them; no discussion until everyone has submitted.
2. **Give each one a distinct code** (for example `R11`, `R12`, `R13`). One person, one code, one submission.
3. **Send** the Google Form link and `REVIEWER_INSTRUCTIONS.md` (same form and packets as round 1; the form is
   generated by `create_l10_form.gs`, unchanged). Remind them: enter only the code, no name or email.
4. **Export** the responses to a NEW csv (do not overwrite `L10 Evidence Review - responses - Form Responses 1.csv`).
5. **Analyse** from the repository root:

   ```
   python results/l10_human_form/analyze_round2.py "<new responses>.csv" results/l10_human_analysis_round2
   ```

   The script refuses duplicate codes and compares the human majority with the frozen v9 reference
   (`results/l10_independent_validation_20261003_v9/trustlens_reference.json`).
6. **Report it as descriptive.** Keep round 1 untouched; add round 2 as a separate table. Then, in the report,
   replace "six of the seven submissions share one reviewer code" with the new independent count, and keep the
   caveat that reviewers are evidence readers, not ground truth.

Not done by the assistant: recruiting people and collecting responses.
