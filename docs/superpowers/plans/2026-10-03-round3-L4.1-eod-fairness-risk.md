# Round 3 — L4.1: binary fairness risk from the equal-opportunity difference (2026-10-03)

Branch `round-3-limitations`. Methodology `v5-binary-fairness-risk-2026` → `v6-eod-fairness-risk-2026`.
The binary fairness evidence is now stamped `tl-fairness-binary-v1.2`. Multiclass
`tl-methodology-v1.0`, `ground_truth.json` and its rules, the FRIES formula, and all
result folders are unchanged.

## Methodology note (read first)

- **v1.1 is exploratory and rejected.** `tl-fairness-binary-v1.1` (commit `d4268fb`)
  used `excess_dpd_v2` as the trigger. That candidate was rejected after inspecting the
  stored 2026-10-02 evidence, because `b_g = P(Ŷ=1|g) − P(Y=1|g) = (TPR_g − 1)·π_g + FPR_g·(1 − π_g)`
  depends directly on the group base rate π_g. The inspection found that the statistic:
  - was non-zero on the clean controls,
  - fell as the injected fairness defect grew,
  - rose with the safety defect.
- **EOD was not pre-registered.** The equal-opportunity difference was chosen *after*
  that inspection. It is not described here as having been pre-registered before it.
- **The frozen suite is exploratory evidence only.** The metric choice was informed by
  its stored results, so it can no longer serve as a confirmatory test of this rule.
  Confirmation needs a fresh fairness validation setup and seed; see
  `2026-10-03-round3-L4.1-confirmatory-prereg.md`.
- **No re-tuning.** ε was not re-tuned. It stays at the existing methodology-wide
  practical tolerance `EPSILON = 0.02`.

## Statistic

Eligibility comes first:
- **Eligible groups:** groups with n ≥ `min_group_n`.
- **Positive labels:** every eligible group needs ≥ `MIN_GROUP_POSITIVES = 1` positive
  label, i.e. TP + FN > 0. Without one, TPR is undefined, not 0. This is the minimum
  required for TPR. Precision beyond that is left to the CI-width gate.

The primary risk statistic is the TPR gap over eligible groups:

```
TPR_g = TP_g / (TP_g + FN_g)
EOD   = max_g TPR_g − min_g TPR_g      (two groups: |TPR_A − TPR_B|)
```

It is stored as `equal_opportunity_difference`. The existing `equalized_odds_difference`,
which is max(TPR gap, FPR gap), is unchanged.

The FPR gap (`fpr_gap`) is **secondary evidence only**. No combined score is created.

## CI and decision rule (frozen)

- **CI:** `eopp_ci` is a bootstrap percentile 95% CI of EOD itself. It uses B = 1000 row
  resamples and the same deterministic seed policy as the other fairness bootstraps
  (contract `seed`, default 42). Groups with n < `min_group_n` are dropped inside each
  replicate. A replicate in which a kept group has no positive label is discarded.

| order | condition | finding | aspect_scoring | risks_triggered |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | < 2 eligible groups (`G-FAIR-N-GROUP`), an eligible group with 0 positives (`G-FAIR-POSITIVES`), or no CI (`G-FAIR-CI-MISSING`) | INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE (probe status INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE) | `not_scored` | `[]` |
| 2 | EOD CI width > `CI_WIDE_THRESHOLD` (0.15): `G-FAIR-CI-WIDE`, flag `wide_ci_eopp` | INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE (status EVALUATED) | `mapping_blocked` | `[]` |
| 3 | EOD > ε and CI lower > ε | FAIRNESS_RISK | `risk_detected` | `["F-FAIR-EOPP"]` |
| 4 | EOD > ε and CI lower ≤ ε | DISPARITY_OBSERVED | `disparity_observed` | `[]` |
| 5 | EOD ≤ ε | NO_MATERIAL_DISPARITY | `no_material_risk` | `[]` |

- **Gate change:** G-FAIR-CI-WIDE now applies to the EOD CI, because that is the CI the
  rule uses. A wide DP/EO/F1 CI only adds a non-blocking `wide_ci_dp`, `wide_ci_eo` or
  `wide_ci_f1` flag. The rest of the gate's semantics (status EVALUATED,
  `mapping_blocked`, confidence 0.75) is unchanged.
- **Evidence-layer only:** `scored_risk_id` stays null. The status reason says the risk
  is "evidence for review, not a finding that the model is unfair".
- **Ground truth:** the frozen evidence-level rule in `ground_truth.json` flags
  FAIRNESS only on FAIRNESS_RISK.

## FRIES

The FRIES formula and the fairness O/S/D band formula are unchanged. The band still
abstains on `mapping_blocked`, `not_scored` and INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE. Because the gate
moved from the DP CI to the EOD CI and a positive-label check was added, an individual
evaluation can now abstain, or stop abstaining, where it did not before.
