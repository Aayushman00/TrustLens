\chapter*{Abstract}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Abstract}
Downloading a classifier from a public model hub takes one line of code, but deciding whether it deserves to be used is harder: the information at hand is a free-text model card, a possibly empty licence field and no standard evidence about behaviour on sensitive groups. \textbf{TrustLens} is a semi-automated, evidence-driven, FRIES-aligned framework that collects this evidence for Hugging Face text classifiers. It imports a model, runs five probes locally (Fairness, Robustness, Integrity, Explainability, Safety), stores hashed evidence, interprets it into Occurrence/Severity/Detection ratings and produces a versioned report. The primary output is a five-dimensional audit profile; the aggregate FRIES score is a secondary heuristic summary.

We validated the system with controlled flawed models, severity sweeps, a pre-registered fairness confirmation, a deterministic-versus-LLM comparison, an independent evidence review and an audit of seven real Hub models. The measured evidence is repeatable (zero spread over five runs) and monotone in injected severity (Spearman $\rho=1.0$ in every sweep). A calibrated O/S/D mapping widened the held-out score range over the injected defects from 0.68--1.18 to 4.60--6.65 points, separating disclosure gaps from risks removed false flags on clean controls, and an equal-opportunity rule detected every injected fairness defect at flip rates of 0.4 and above on fresh data (it also flagged the real reference model). Artifact integrity checks detected a one-byte change in a demonstration, and all seven audited Hub models verified against the Hub's hashes. Independent reviewers agree with TrustLens on risk for 6 of 12 packets and on evidence sufficiency for 9 of 12, and seven real models received descriptive profiles without ranking.

TrustLens does not certify any model; it is meant to help a human reviewer see where the evidence is strong and where it is thin.

\textbf{Keywords:} model auditing, trustworthy AI, FRIES, Hugging Face Hub, fairness, equal opportunity, artifact integrity, model cards, evidence-driven evaluation.
